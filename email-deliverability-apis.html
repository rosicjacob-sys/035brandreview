2The 4 Distinct Questions "Email Deliverability Tracking" Actually Answers

No single API answers all 4 questions. Understanding these 4 layers explains why we combine native server hooks + seed-list APIs + RapidAPI utilities.

Layer A: Server & SMTP Delivery Telemetry ("Did the receiving mail server accept or bounce it?")
  • What it measures: Sent count, SMTP `250 OK` acceptance, Hard Bounces (`550 User unknown`), Soft Bounces (`421 Too many connections` / `Mailbox full`), Deferrals (throttling by Gmail/Yahoo), and Feedback Loop (ARF) spam complaints.
  • Best API: **NEVER use a third-party wrapper here!** Your `037` worker already ingests native webhooks directly from **KumoMTA (`POST /api/kumo/logs`)**, **Amazon SES (`SNS -> SQS`)**, and **Instantly (`POST /api/webhooks/instantly`)**.
Layer B: Inbox vs. Spam vs. Promotions Placement ("Once accepted, which folder did it land in?")
  • Why SMTP logs can't tell you this: When Gmail accepts your email with `250 2.0.0 OK` and silently puts it into the **Spam folder**, KumoMTA and SES still report `"Delivered"`!
  • How it's measured: Only by sending the email to **Seed Mailboxes** (real Gmail, Workspace, Outlook 365, Hotmail, Yahoo, iCloud accounts) and reading their IMAP folders (`INBOX` vs `Spam` vs `Promotions`).
  • Best APIs to cross-check: **1) Instantly Placement API** + **2) GlockApps API** + **3) EmailGuard / MailReach** + **4) Your own Sentinel IMAP Fleet** ( vs. **Validity Everest** at $1k+/mo).
Layer C: Domain & IP Reputation / Blacklists ("Are Gmail, Microsoft, or Spamhaus mad at our IPs/domains?")
  • What it measures: Real user spam-button rate on Gmail (`0.0%` vs `0.3%` danger line), IP/Domain reputation tier (`High / Medium / Low / Bad`), and listings on 100+ RBL blocklists.
  • Best APIs: **Google Postmaster Tools API** (Free, built into `037`) + **Microsoft SNDS** (Free) + **Spamhaus DQS API** + **HetrixTools API / RapidAPI RBL Checkers**.
Layer D: Pre-Send List Hygiene & Content Scoring ("Will this contact bounce or will this HTML trigger spam filters?")
  • What it measures: Invalid mailboxes, catch-all domains, disposable emails, honey-pot spam traps, and SpamAssassin / HTML lint scores.
  • Best APIs: **RapidAPI Multi-Verifier** + **MillionVerifier / ZeroBounce / NeverBounce API** + your built-in `packages/templates` 19-rule deterministic analyzer.

3What Is "Validity Everest" — And When Is It Worth It vs. Overkill?

You asked about "Everest" (which Jerome's bot also mentioned in `08-email-jerome` and which is documented in `037-email-marketing/docs/infra-test-plan/index.html §14`).

What Validity Everest Actually Is:
Validity Everest (`everest.validity.com`, formerly **Return Path** + **250ok** + **BriteVerify**) is the legacy enterprise standard for Fortune 500 email deliverability. It combines:
  1. Seed-List Inbox Placement Panel: A list of ~70+ ISP seed addresses (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Comcast, corporate filters like Proofpoint/Mimecast). You send your campaign to their seed list, and their API (`https://api.everest.validity.com/api/2.0/...`) returns the Inbox % vs. Spam % vs. Missing % per ISP.
  2. Sender Score & Reputation Monitoring: Tracks IP reputation, spam traps (from Validity's proprietary spam-trap network), and DMARC reports.
  3. BriteVerify List Cleaning: Built-in per-email verification credits.

Why You Should NOT Rely Only on Everest (and Why We Recommend Testing 3 Lighter APIs First)

  • 1. Cost & Contract Lock-In: Validity Everest costs **$725 to $1,500+/month** on an annual enterprise contract (as noted in your `037` test plan: *"~$1,000+/month per 2026 reviews; doesn't pay for itself under ~100k–500k sends/month"*).
  • 2. The "Static Seed List" Blind Spot for Cold Email: Enterprise seed panels like Everest use **passive seed mailboxes** that never reply, never click, and receive thousands of marketing blasts every day. Modern Gmail and Microsoft 365 spam filters weight **recipient engagement and sender-recipient history** heavily — meaning Everest's static seeds can sometimes say `"95% Inbox"` while real cold prospects get routed to Spam, OR say `"Spam"` on a cold-email domain simply because the seed never interacted with you!
  • 3. Better Strategy for Your Operation: Instead of paying $1,000+/mo for one monolithic provider, you get **much higher accuracy** for **~$59–$129/mo total** by cross-checking **three independent placement & reputation sources** side-by-side (Section 4 & 5 below)!

4The Truth About RapidAPI for Email Deliverability: What Exists on RapidAPI (and What Doesn't)

You asked if RapidAPI has good options so you can easily test multiple providers with one RapidAPI key.

Crucial Distinction on RapidAPI:
• **What RapidAPI DOES Have (Great to Test Multi-Provider):** RapidAPI has dozens of APIs for **1) Email Address Verification / Catch-All / Disposable Detection**, **2) IP & Domain Blacklist (RBL) Multi-Checking (100+ lists)**, **3) DNS / SPF / DKIM / DMARC / BIMI Validation**, and **4) SpamAssassin / Content Spam-Score Analysis**.
• **What RapidAPI Does NOT Have:** RapidAPI does **not** host true multi-ISP **Inbox Placement Seed-List Panels** (because a seed test requires sending an actual SMTP email to 50–100 live Gmail/Outlook/Yahoo mailboxes and waiting 2–5 minutes for an IMAP worker to check which folder it landed in — which doesn't fit a synchronous 1-second RapidAPI GET request).
