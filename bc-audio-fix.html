Last Updated: 2026-11-02 00:11:56  |  Cost: 0 credits (local FFmpeg audio remux)  |  Scope: Tier B (V05..V08) & Tier C (V09..V20) across all 8 Golden characters (Tier A untouched per instruction).

Root Cause Fixed: _run_20x_fast.py previously hardcoded rate = 48000 instead of probing E.sample_rate(master_p). Because 67/83 master videos use 22,050 Hz audio and 16/83 use 44,100 Hz audio, asetrate=48000*speed played 22.05 kHz tracks at 1.85x–2.40x speed (chipmunk voice followed by ~5s of silence) and 44.1 kHz tracks +8.84% too fast. All 1,328 unique B & C variants (1,776 character placements) have now been re-rendered with their exact native master sample rate (22,050 Hz or 44,100 Hz), verified at 0 mismatched sample rates and 0 trailing silence.
